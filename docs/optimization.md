### Task
Add multi-threading to the “lock” function of the earlier implemented “Tree of Space” problem with granular locking on the path. Of-course, you must design a solution keeping in mind that it’s fit for unlock and upgrade functions too in future.

### Rule
No libraries allowed. Implement everything from scratch.

Questions fired non-stop: OS concepts (locks, deadlocks, race conditions, contention), dry runs, edge cases, real-time simulation.

The interviewers were really very friendly throughout. He guided me through tricky parts without spoon-feeding, and we had good back-and-forth discussions on different locking strategies (fine-grained per node/path and coarser approaches).
